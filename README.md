# 📊 EDA on Retail Sales Data

## 📌 Project Overview

This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on retail/e-commerce sales data to understand sales trends, customer behaviour, product performance, and revenue patterns.

The analysis uses Python and popular data analysis and visualization libraries to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Understand the structure and quality of the sales dataset.
- Perform data cleaning and preprocessing.
- Calculate descriptive statistics.
- Analyze monthly and quarterly sales trends.
- Study customer demographics such as age and gender.
- Identify the top 10 best-selling products.
- Analyze revenue generated by product categories.
- Examine relationships between numerical variables using correlation analysis.
- Generate additional business insights.
- Provide actionable business recommendations.

---

## 📂 Dataset

The dataset used in this project is:

`realistic_e_commerce_sales_data.csv`

The dataset contains 1,000 sales records and includes information related to:

- Customer ID
- Gender
- Region
- Age
- Product Name
- Category
- Unit Price
- Quantity
- Total Price
- Shipping Fee
- Shipping Status
- Order Date

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 1. Data Inspection
Examined dataset shape, columns, data types, missing values, and duplicate records.

### 2. Data Cleaning
Handled missing values, converted order dates into the appropriate datetime format, and checked for duplicate records.

### 3. Descriptive Statistics
Calculated:
- Mean
- Median
- Mode
- Standard deviation
- Summary statistics

### 4. Sales Trend Analysis
Analyzed:
- Monthly sales trends
- Quarterly sales trends

### 5. Customer Demographics
Analyzed:
- Customer age groups
- Gender distribution

### 6. Product Performance
Identified the top 10 best-selling products based on quantity sold.

### 7. Category Revenue Analysis
Compared revenue generated by different product categories.

### 8. Correlation Analysis
Created a correlation heatmap to understand relationships among numerical variables.

### 9. Additional Insight
Compared average order value across customer gender groups to identify differences in purchasing value.

---

## 📈 Key Findings

The analysis provides insights into:

- Overall sales performance
- Monthly and quarterly sales patterns
- Customer demographic distribution
- Best-performing products
- Revenue-generating product categories
- Relationships between numerical sales variables
- Differences in average order value across customer groups

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the EDA, businesses can:

1. **Prioritize high-performing products** by maintaining sufficient inventory and promoting products with strong sales volume.

2. **Focus on high-revenue categories** by allocating appropriate inventory, marketing, and promotional resources to categories contributing significantly to revenue.

3. **Use customer demographic insights** to create more targeted marketing campaigns and improve customer engagement.
